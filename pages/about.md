**Hi there!** My name is Diogo and I'm a Ph.D. student at the <span style="color:#62b775">**Max Plank Institute for Extraterrestrial Physics**</span> in the Galactic Center group led by Prof. Reinhard Genzel. I work at the interface of theory and experiment using the Galactic Center as a laboratory to test our understanding of gravity. 

My research interests range from the dynamics of nuclear star clusters to the accretion flow physics near Supermassive Black Holes. In particular, I model the observations of stars and gas around the Supermassive Black hole at the center of our galaxy, Sagittarius A*.
